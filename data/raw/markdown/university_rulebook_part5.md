35. Course Requirements
35.1 Course Information

At the beginning of each semester, departments should provide students with information about registered courses.

Course information may include learning outcomes, assessment methods, attendance expectations, laboratory requirements, recommended resources, and important academic dates.

Students should review the information provided for each registered course.

35.2 Course Prerequisites

Courses may have prerequisite subjects or other academic requirements.

A prerequisite requirement must be satisfied before registration unless an authorized exception has been granted.

35.3 Course Capacity

Courses may have enrollment limits because of classroom capacity, laboratory resources, faculty availability, or programme requirements.

Where a course is oversubscribed, the university may apply an allocation process.

35.4 Course Changes

The university may modify course schedules, instructors, rooms, or other operational details when necessary.

Students should rely on official notices for current course information.

36. Laboratory and Practical Work
36.1 Laboratory Participation

Students enrolled in courses containing laboratory components are expected to participate in scheduled laboratory sessions.

Laboratory attendance may be recorded separately from lecture attendance.

36.2 Laboratory Safety

Students must comply with laboratory safety instructions.

Students may be required to use appropriate protective equipment and follow instructions issued by laboratory personnel.

36.3 Laboratory Records

Students may be required to maintain laboratory records, reports, logbooks, or other documentation specified by the course.

36.4 Missed Laboratory

A student who misses a compulsory laboratory should notify the relevant instructor as soon as reasonably possible.

A replacement laboratory may be permitted where resources and academic requirements allow.

Students should not assume that a replacement session is automatically available.

37. Academic Projects
37.1 Project Registration

Where a programme requires an academic project, students must register the project through the process specified by their department.

Project registration may require approval of a project topic and supervisor.

37.2 Project Supervision

A project supervisor may guide students regarding project scope, methodology, documentation, and academic requirements.

Students remain responsible for the work submitted under their name.

37.3 Project Submission

Projects must be submitted by the deadline specified by the department.

A submission may be rejected or penalized where it does not satisfy applicable formatting or submission requirements.

37.4 Project Evaluation

Project evaluation may consider technical work, documentation, presentation, methodology, originality, and other criteria specified by the programme.

38. Internship and Industry Training
38.1 Internship Approval

Students undertaking an internship as part of an academic programme must obtain approval through the designated department process where required.

38.2 Internship Documentation

Students may be required to submit internship offer letters, joining information, completion certificates, reports, evaluations, or other documents.

38.3 Academic Credit

Where an internship carries academic credit, the student must satisfy the applicable academic requirements.

Completion of an internship alone does not guarantee academic credit unless all programme requirements are satisfied.

38.4 Internship Conduct

Students representing the university during an internship are expected to comply with university requirements and the reasonable policies of the host organization.

39. Attendance Monitoring and Warnings
39.1 Monitoring

Academic departments should monitor attendance throughout the semester.

Attendance information may be used to identify students who are at risk of falling below the applicable attendance requirement.

39.2 Warning Threshold

A department may issue an attendance warning when a student's attendance falls below 80%.

The purpose of an attendance warning is to alert the student that continued absence may result in examination ineligibility.

39.3 Student Response

A student receiving an attendance warning should review the attendance record and raise any apparent errors promptly.

Students should maintain supporting evidence for approved leave or other authorized absences.

39.4 Final Eligibility

The examination eligibility decision is made after attendance records and applicable exceptions have been reviewed.

A warning should not be interpreted as a guarantee of examination eligibility.

40. Examination Administration
40.1 Examination Registration

Students must complete examination registration using the process specified by the examination office.

Registration may require payment of the applicable examination fee.

40.2 Examination Admit Document

The examination office may issue an admit card, examination authorization, or other document confirming examination details.

Students should verify the examination date, time, course, and venue before the examination.

40.3 Examination Venue

Students must attend the examination at the venue specified by the examination office.

A student who attends the wrong venue may not be permitted to take the examination.

40.4 Identity Verification

Students may be required to present university identification or another approved identity document.

The examination authority may verify the identity of a student before permitting entry.

40.5 Seating

Students must occupy the seat assigned or approved by the examination authority.

Students should follow instructions issued by invigilators regarding seating arrangements.

41. Examination Results
41.1 Result Publication

Examination results are published through the university's authorized academic records or results system.

Students should use official university systems when checking results.

41.2 Result Corrections

Where a student believes that a published result contains an administrative error, the student may submit a correction request through the prescribed process.

41.3 Revaluation

Where a revaluation or review facility is provided for a particular examination, eligible students may apply within the published deadline.

The applicable notice should specify the subjects, fees, deadlines, and procedure.

41.4 Supplementary Examination

The university may conduct supplementary or repeat examinations where authorized by the applicable academic regulations.

Eligibility for such examinations is determined according to the relevant regulations.

42. Graduation Requirements
42.1 Programme Completion

A student may graduate only after satisfying all academic and administrative requirements of the applicable programme.

42.2 Academic Requirements

Graduation requirements may include:

successful completion of required courses;
satisfaction of credit requirements;
completion of required projects or internships;
satisfaction of minimum academic performance requirements; and
completion of other programme-specific requirements.
42.3 Administrative Clearance

Students may be required to obtain clearance from relevant university offices before graduation.

Clearance may include library, finance, hostel, laboratory, or other applicable offices.

42.4 Graduation Application

Students must submit graduation applications within the announced period where an application is required.

Late applications may be processed only when permitted by the university.

43. Student Identification
43.1 University Identification Card

Students may be issued an official university identification card.

Students should carry the identification card when required for academic, examination, hostel, library, or administrative purposes.

43.2 Lost Identification Card

A student who loses an identification card should report the loss through the designated university process.

The university may issue a replacement subject to applicable requirements or fees.

43.3 Misuse

A student must not lend an identification card to another person or use another person's identification card.

Misuse may result in disciplinary action.

44. Library Regulations
44.1 Library Membership

Eligible students may use university library facilities subject to library regulations.

44.2 Borrowing

Students may borrow materials within the borrowing limits applicable to their category.

The borrowing period and renewal rules may vary by material type.

44.3 Overdue Materials

Students must return borrowed materials by the applicable due date.

The library may impose restrictions or charges for overdue materials where authorized.

44.4 Lost Materials

A student who loses borrowed material should report the loss to the library.

The university may require replacement of the material or payment of an applicable replacement cost.

45. Information Technology Resources
45.1 Acceptable Use

University computing resources must be used primarily for authorized academic, administrative, research, or other university-related purposes.

45.2 Account Security

Students are responsible for protecting their university account credentials.

Students should not share passwords or authentication information with other persons.

45.3 Unauthorized Access

Students must not attempt to access systems, accounts, files, or information without authorization.

Attempts to bypass security controls may be treated as misconduct.

45.4 Network Use

Students must use university network resources responsibly.

Activities that materially interfere with university systems or violate applicable regulations may result in access restrictions or disciplinary action.

45.5 Reporting Security Issues

Students who identify a potential security vulnerability or unauthorized access should report it to the designated university technology office.

Students should not exploit a vulnerability after discovering it.

46. Communication and Student Queries
46.1 Academic Queries

Students should direct academic queries to the relevant instructor, programme coordinator, department, or academic office depending on the nature of the issue.

46.2 Administrative Queries

Questions concerning fees, records, hostel matters, examinations, or student services should be directed to the appropriate administrative office.

46.3 Response Time

University offices should make reasonable efforts to respond to student queries within their operational capacity.

Response times may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the request.

46.4 Escalation

When a student does not receive a satisfactory response, the matter may be escalated through the appropriate administrative or academic hierarchy.

Students should preserve relevant correspondence and supporting documents.

47. Policy Hierarchy
47.1 University Regulations

University-wide regulations establish the general requirements applicable across programmes and departments.

47.2 Programme Regulations

Programme-specific regulations may establish additional requirements for students enrolled in that programme.

47.3 Department Procedures

Departments may publish procedures for implementing university or programme requirements.

Procedures must be consistent with applicable regulations.

47.4 Specific Exceptions

An exception to a general rule is valid only when it is authorized by a competent authority and supported by the applicable regulation or formal decision.

47.5 Unresolved Inconsistency

When two published rules appear to impose different requirements and neither rule clearly establishes precedence, the matter should be referred to the appropriate interpretation authority.

Students should not assume that the more favorable requirement automatically applies.
